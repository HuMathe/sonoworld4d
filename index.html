Abstract

Recent progress in visual scene generation has extended beyond static 3D scenes toward the more challenging problem of generating dynamic 4D scenes from minimal input. However, sound, despite being intrinsically coupled with dynamic objects and events, remains largely unmodeled in prior work. We introduce Video-to-4DAVScene, a new task that turns a video into an explorable, time-varying 4D audio-visual scene, and present the SonoWorld4D framework for this task. Given a monocular video, our method generates a 360° static background, reconstructs explicit motion trajectories of dynamic, sound-emitting foreground objects, and synthesizes a dynamic sound field consistent across multiple sources through dual-space diffusion synchronization. The resulting 4D scene supports real-time novel-view visual and spatial audio rendering. Experiments on real-world dynamic scenes and a user study demonstrate that SonoWorld4D generates 4D audio-visual scenes with perceptually realistic, physically grounded spatial audio consistent with scene geometry and visual content.

Audio-space mapping demo · PlaneReal audio · source / camera / inverse

Hear how diffusion synchronization moves audio between spaces.

At every sampling step, diffusion synchronization decodes clean-audio predictions in both object space and camera space, then maps them between the two and fuses the results. This demo lets you listen to the forward and inverse rendering operations that make the exchange possible — using a real plane fly-over as the source.
